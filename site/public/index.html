Built on ComfyUI and open models

The open source video studio

Restyle footage, swap the people, change the place, animate a photo or turn one product shot into a finished ad. Open models, on your own GPU or Comfy Cloud. Every result opens in ComfyUI.

Get Openfield Quickstart 
# macOS, Linux or Windows, with uv and FFmpeg installed
git clone https://github.com/AIPowerGrid/openfield && cd openfield
COMFY_API_KEY=your-key uv run openfield --cloud

Runs on Comfy Cloud with one key, or on your own ComfyUI worker with --backend self-hosted. MIT licensed.

Transform a video

Six ways to start

Every card on the Openfield home screen is a finished example that opens the path that made it. Nothing renders until you press Generate, and each button shows its cost first.

Product adCreate a product adOne bottle photo becomes freeze, trail and wireframe shots with a closing card and music. FLUX.2 Klein places the real product; LTX-2.5 animates it.
Photo to videoAnimate a photoA still becomes a moving shot with a camera move, a trained effect or a bullet-time orbit.
Text to videoIdea to videoDescribe a scene; get a 5, 10 or 15 second shot with generated sound.
Audio to videoShot from audioBring a voice or a track; the shot is timed to it. Grieg's Hall of the Mountain King becomes fireworks on the beat.
ShortMulti-shot storyPlan a hook, reveal and payoff on a shot board, audition looks, then export one edited short with captions and music.
Video to videoTransform a videoUpload footage and restyle it with a saved style or your own words. Motion, timing and sound stay yours, and a whole clip can be restyled in 10 second windows.

Edit who and what is in the shot

The features people reach for in a closed studio, done with open models. Each runs on a clip or a photo you already loaded and exports the exact ComfyUI graph that made it.

Swap the personPut someone else into the clip from one photo. Their moves and voice stay.From a video
New backgroundDescribe a new place in words, or drop a photo of one. The subject stays put.From a video
Remove an objectName what should go. It is found, masked and painted out across the clip.From a video
Add an objectDrop a product or prop from a photo into the scene, like a bottle in a hand.From a video
Bullet-time orbitFreeze the moment in a photo and fly the camera all the way around it.From a photo
FPV fly-throughA drone-style dive into a still photo.From a photo
Make them speakType a line; the person in the photo says it with a matching voice and lips. Or change what someone in a clip says, in their own voice.From a photo or clip
Four versionsRender the same shot at four seeds, side by side, and keep the best one.Any take

Also in the box: 27 one-click effects, camera-move presets, FPS boost, background removal, 2× upscale, depth maps, +5 s extend, reframe to vertical, auto captions and generated music. Each tool exports a ComfyUI bundle with the graph, inputs and model list.

Styles that keep your motion

Transform styles pair a FLUX.2 Klein look with LTX-2.5 and Union Control, so the source footage drives the result frame by frame. Pick one, or save your own.

Open models, official SDK

Openfield submits every job through the official Comfy SDK, to Comfy Cloud or to a ComfyUI worker you run. No hosted black boxes.

LTX-2.5Video generation and video-to-video with Union Control, 5 to 15 s, with sound.
FLUX.2 KleinOpening looks, product placement and new backgrounds from words.
MiniMax H3Person swap, bullet-time orbit, FPV flight and speech from text.
SAM 3.1 + BiRefNetFind, mask and cut out people and objects before an edit.
LTX-2 effects27 trained effect LoRAs: cakeify, time-lapse, outfit check and more.
